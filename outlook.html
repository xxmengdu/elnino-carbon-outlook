NASA / ISS · 16 Jan 2026 · Use terms

Research forecast for July–December 2026, based on climate forecasts initialized on 1 June. The event is still developing.

The outlook identifies two distinct pathways of carbon risk: weakening land carbon sinks in eastern South America and elevated fire activity in equatorial Asia. Regional losses can be substantial even when gains elsewhere partly offset the global total.

The Pacific signal, the land response

Satellite observations show above-average sea-surface heights across the central and eastern equatorial Pacific. Warmer water expands, making sea level a useful indicator of the developing event.

Our research asks what the accompanying changes in heat and rainfall could mean for carbon uptake, ecosystem respiration and fire during July–December.

This is a June ocean observation. The land-carbon results are research projections, not satellite observations.
