Run, check & publish

Run the carbon and fire outlooks every two months. Publish checked results for the complete forecast months.

When a run can start

ECMWF: cycles are initialized on the first of every month; products are scheduled for dissemination on the fifth at 12:00 UTC. Our plan selects the October, December and February cycles.

ERA5T: the preliminary ERA5 release is updated daily with about a five-day delay. The preceding month's final day is usually available around the fifth; exact availability varies.

Each planned run waits for both inputs to be complete. Results are published after the model runs and checks; the fifth is an input-availability estimate, not a publication deadline.
